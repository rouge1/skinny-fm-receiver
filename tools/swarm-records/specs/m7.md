# Phase m7 spec: m5 broke the carrier class on a real DVR

Goal: a pure CW tone at low SNR in a wide recording classes `carrier` again. Found by the planner re-timing the
real DVR after m5: speed is right (17 to 18 s for 240 million samples, 55 MB anonymous memory) but the class
went from `carrier` (m4) to `fsk`: `frequency.levels_hz` [100030, 100329] for a tone at exactly +100000 Hz,
`keyed: true`, symbol rate 1140 baud, with the envelope constant and one tone. The file: 28 MS/s, 8.6 s,
one CW tone at 19 dB SNR over the 28 MHz (white noise), VSG60 -60 dBm at +100 kHz from the centre. m4's code on
the same file: levels [100037], deviation 78 Hz, carrier. The m5 tests (40 dB SNR, 1 s) missed it. Likely
cause to check first: m5's overlap-save/phasor-sum band-limit puts a phase or gain step at block edges, or its
decimation aliases noise, which the instantaneous frequency turns into a second cluster. Do not assume.
Read `CONTRACTS-measure.md` (the class rules, "Where the envelope and frequency are measured"), `CLAUDE.md`,
`AGENTS.md`.

First command of the manager: `swarm.py phase m7 active`. Register every task WITH its model
(`swarm.py task m7 <task> queued --model <m>`); never without `--model`.

## Behaviour (what the new tests assert; they fail on main now)
- CW at +100 kHz plus a fraction (+100003.3 Hz), 28 MS/s, 3 s, white complex noise so the SNR over the
  full 28 MHz is 19 dB: class `carrier`, `frequency.keyed` False, `frequency.deviation_hz` under 100 Hz, one
  tone. The same signal at 10 MS/s and at 1 MS/s (SNR 19 dB over the band): the same.
- The same CW at SNR 40 dB, 28 MS/s (the m5 test) still `carrier`; and the 127+6 existing checks pass:
  OOK, FSK (9.6 kbaud, +/-5 kHz), FM, AM, noise, two-tone are classed as before.
- The frequency levels do not depend on block size: measuring the same file with the internal block size
  doubled and halved (a module constant or argument, whichever exists) gives `levels_hz` within 20 Hz.
- No whole-span array and the file read at most twice (the m5 tests still pass); 28 MS/s, 1 s CW under 4 s.
