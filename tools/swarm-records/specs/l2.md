# Phase l2 spec: LTE verification, the SSS check and the physical cell ID

Goal: `iqlte.fingerprint` returns the physical cell ID (PCI = 3 * N_ID(1) + N_ID(2)) of an LTE FDD downlink, with a
chance estimate, and says which half-frame the SSS came from (so the 10 ms frame boundary). This is check 4 of the
plan in `knowledge/roadmap.md`, Round 25. The PSS root (N_ID(2)) already exists (phase l1). Numpy only in
`iqlte.py`. Nothing here decodes broadcast data (MIB/SIB1 are stage 5): the SSS is a fixed sequence function of the
cell ID, a classification feature.

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `tools/fm_receiver/iqlte.py` (the l1 winner: `_pss_on_grid`, `_symbol_bodies`,
`_bodies_at`, how PSS timing is found and how chances are made), `tools/tests/lte_synth.py` (the generator's SSS and
PSS), `tools/tests/test_iqlte.py` and `tools/tests/lte_negatives.py`.

## Method (what the SSS check does; the implementer chooses details)
Find the PSS as `pss_search` does (timing, root N_ID(2), CFO). In FDD the SSS is in the symbol immediately before the
PSS symbol, in slots 0 and 10, on the same central 62 subcarriers. Take the FFT of the SSS symbol and of the PSS
symbol at each PSS occurrence; use the PSS (known) to estimate the channel on those subcarriers and equalise the SSS;
correlate against the 168 x 2 hypotheses (N_ID(1) 0-167, and subframe 0 or 5, which swaps the two interleaved
m-sequences, 36.211 6.11.2.1); combine the statistic over all the half-frames in the capture. The strongest hypothesis
gives N_ID(1) and the half-frame; the chance comes from the other hypotheses' scores in the same capture (the null),
multiplied by the number of hypotheses, floored at 1e-300. The carrier is assumed centred (as in l1).

## Interfaces
- `iqlte.sss_search(iq, fs) -> dict(ran, nid1, nid2, pci, half_frame (0 or 1), frame_offset_samples, score, chance,
  halfframes_used, detail)`. `pci` is None when the chance is above 1e-3 or the PSS found nothing.
- `iqlte.fingerprint`: the `sss` check now has `ran: True`; `pci` is the SSS's pci or None; `verdict` is unchanged
  by the SSS (still decided by the CP and PSS checks); if SSS gives a pci whose N_ID(2) differs from the PSS root,
  the verdict drops to `possible` and the detail says so.
- Short or under-rate captures still return `unknown` with no exception.

## Acceptance targets (measured by `test_iqlte.py`, printed as ok/FAIL lines)
- A: 10 MHz, native rate, 10 frames; PCI in {0, 1, 2, 3, 123, 250, 251, 500, 503}; SNR in {20, 0, -5} dB; seeds 0-2
  (81 cases): `pci` equal to the generated PCI.
- B: bandwidths 1.4, 3, 5, 15, 20 MHz at 20 dB, PCI 301, seeds 0-1 (10 cases).
- C: 10 MHz resampled to 20 MS/s, CFO +2 kHz, SNR 10 dB, PCI 307, seeds 0-4 (5 cases).
- D: negatives (noise, tone, wifi_like, atsc_like, uplink_like), seeds 0-4 (25 cases): `pci is None`.
- E: controls: SSS zeroed in the generator (a new `sss=False` option) at 20 dB, seeds 0-4: `pci is None` and the
  `pss` check still passes; a capture shorter than 20 ms: no exception.
- F: `half_frame` and `frame_offset_samples` equal the generator's, in the cases of A at 20 dB.
- G: time for `fingerprint` on 0.1 s at 15.36 MS/s.

## Gates (pre-registered: fixed before any worker runs)
- G1 A: exact PCI in 81 of 81 at 20 dB and 0 dB (54 cases); at -5 dB at least 24 of 27, and no wrong PCI with
  `chance <= 1e-3` (a wrong answer must be flagged as weak).
- G2 B: exact PCI in 10 of 10.
- G3 C: exact PCI in 5 of 5.
- G4 D: `pci is None` in 25 of 25.
- G5 E: `sss=False` controls give `pci is None` in 5 of 5 with the PSS check still passing; the short capture raises
  nothing in 3 of 3.
- G6 F: the half-frame and the frame offset (within 2 samples at the capture's rate) correct in all the 27 cases at
  20 dB of A.
- G7 G: under 5 s on this machine (load below 0.75 x cores).
- G8 `iqlte.py` imports only numpy and the standard library; no file outside the ownership table changes; every
  existing suite and `test_iqlte.py` pass (`run_all.py` excepting only failures the manager shows also fail on
  `lte-base` itself, listed by name).
- G9 audit: every deviation the audit lists is either fixed in `lte_synth.py` (and the tests re-run) or shown by the
  manager to be a misreading by the auditor (cited clause).
- G10 (the planner, not delegated, real air): on the recorded 751 MHz capture, shifted to centre, 5 non-overlapping
  0.2 s segments each give the same `pci`, the PCI's N_ID(2) equals the PSS root (2) and `chance <= 1e-6` in 5 of 5.
  A detector built from the same misreading as the generator cannot pass this. On the recorded ATSC capture:
  `pci is None`.

  wrong turns 15.

  review items. If none is eligible, the best gets one fix round; if it still fails, report.
