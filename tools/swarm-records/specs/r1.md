# Phase r1 spec: research, more broadcast metadata from cell towers

Goal: three workers research, from different lenses, what further metadata cell towers broadcast to every
phone (beyond the LTE MIB and SIB1 this project already decodes) and how to read it from an IQ recording; a
fourth run checks and merges them. The planner turns the merged answer into a plan for the user. Nothing is
implemented or merged in this phase.

Scope rule (the user's, binding on every prompt and answer): broadcast tower metadata only, the kind any phone
receives before it attaches (MIB, SIBs, synchronisation signals, broadcast emergency alerts, cell lists). Never
user traffic, calls, paging contents aimed at individual phones, identities of subscribers, or anything that
needs a phone to attach. Real cell identifiers (PCI, PLMN, TAC, cell ID) never appear in prompts or answers.

Context the workers get in their prompt (public facts about this repo): a BB60D receiver (up to 40 MS/s IQ,
9 kHz-6 GHz), recordings as cf32; `tools/fm_receiver/iqlte.py` (numpy only) finds LTE cells (PSS, SSS, PCI,
CRS, FDD/TDD); `iq-lte --decode` hands a centred 10 or 15 MHz block to an outside srsRAN 4G `pdsch_ue` (AGPL,
built outside the repo) for MIB and SIB1, parsed with pycrate. Finding on real air: two 700 MHz carriers whose
MIB decodes at 9-11 dB but which send no SI-RNTI and no P-RNTI DCI in any subframe (likely carrier-aggregation
secondary carriers), so their operator cannot be read from them.

## Tasks and file ownership
| task | model | edits only | lens (prompt) |
|---|---|---|---|
| lte-sibs | grokw (`--kind research`: repo read, web search and fetch, no edits) | nothing | `prompts/research-r1-a.md`: LTE beyond SIB1 (SIB2-SIB24), how to schedule and decode them from a recording, and how an operator of a carrier with no SIB1 can be found from other cells' broadcasts |
| nr-other | museweb (OpenCode, OpenRouter `:online` web search, read-only `swarm-research` agent) | nothing | `prompts/research-r1-b.md`: 5G NR broadcast (SSB, MIB, SIB1, other SIBs; NSA cells), NB-IoT and LTE-M broadcast, and any 2G/3G still on air in the US |
| tools-fit | haiku (Claude subagent, web tools) | nothing | `prompts/research-r1-c.md`: open-source decoders and offline pipelines that read broadcast data from IQ files, and which fit this setup (BB60D rates, srsRAN outside the repo, numpy-only repo code) |
| consensus | grokw (`--kind research`) | nothing | `prompts/research-r1-consensus.md` with the three answers pasted in; checks claims on the web, merges, ranks |

Runs happen in detached worktrees of `main` (`wt r1 <name> --detach main`). Answers are saved with
`--text-out data/research/r1-<x>.md`. The Haiku run is an Agent-tool subagent: the manager saves its answer to
`data/research/r1-c.md` and logs it with `swarm.py log-run`. The manager pastes the three answers into the
consensus prompt (a worker may not read outside its worktree) and saves the result to
`data/research/r1-consensus.md`.

## Answer format (every research run)
Markdown with these sections, in order:
- `## Items`: one row per piece of broadcast metadata: what it is | which RAT and message (e.g. LTE SIB5) |
  what it tells us (one line) | how to get it from a recording (tool, and what has to be known first) |
  effort (small, medium, large) | confidence (checked in a source, or from memory)
- `## Tools`: name | what it decodes | input (live radio or file) | licence | URL
- `## Our two carriers with no SIB1`: how their operator could be found from broadcast data only
- `## Out of scope seen`: anything found that touches user traffic or identities, named only so it is avoided
- `## Sources`: URLs (3GPP spec numbers count as sources)

## Acceptance targets
- Three research files and one consensus file exist and follow the format.
- The consensus ranks at least five next steps for this repo, each with its tool and effort.

## Gates (pre-registered: fixed before any worker runs)
- G1 three research answers saved under `data/research/`, each with the five sections above (denominator 3). A
  malformed answer is re-run once in its session, not edited.
- G2 the consensus file exists, has the five sections plus `## Ranked next steps` (at least 5 rows), and marks
  every kept claim checked (a URL or a 3GPP clause) or unchecked.
- G3 no answer recommends decoding user traffic, paging aimed at a phone, or subscriber identities, except
  under "Out of scope seen" (the manager greps for this and reports any hit).
- G4 `git status` on main is clean after the phase apart from the committed spec, and no worktree has changes.
- No bake-off, no winner rule: answers are merged, not ranked.
