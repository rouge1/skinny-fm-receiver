# Phase m5 spec: a measurement of a long, wide file must be cheap

Goal: `iq-measure` on a 28 MS/s, 8.6 s IQ DVR (240 million samples; the planner's real file) takes under 25 s
and under 600 MB of extra memory. After m4 it took 61 s and 1.05 GB (it took 12 s before m4): the band-limit
works on the whole array. Read `CONTRACTS-measure.md` (the last section, "Cost of a measurement", and the
tones rule: 8 bins apart, amended 2026-10-14 to match the code), `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`.

First command of the manager: `swarm.py phase m5 active`. Register every task WITH its model
(`swarm.py task m5 <task> queued --model <m>`); never without `--model`.

## Behaviour
- The results do not change: for every signal of the existing suite (127 checks) the card is the same class
  and the numbers agree within their existing tolerances. `measure` of an array and `measure_file` of the same
  samples agree.
- The envelope, frequency, burst and symbol-rate steps work on a narrow band taken by mixing to the occupied
  centre and decimating in chunks (a polyphase or windowed-FIR or FFT-overlap-save per chunk; numpy only, no
  scipy, as the contract says), so the working array is the decimated signal, not the whole span.
- No single `IQSource.read` request is larger than 2**22 samples; no array of the span's full length is made.
- The file is read at most twice.

## Acceptance targets (numbers)
- A synthetic 28 MS/s, 1 s CW tone (+100 kHz, 40 dB SNR, 28 million samples) through `measure_file` from a
  `.cfile`: under 4 s, peak extra memory under 400 MB (the test measures `tracemalloc` or `resource` ru_maxrss
  growth in a subprocess), class `carrier`.
- The largest `read` request seen by a counting source is at most 2**22 samples (a test).
- 30 s of 1 MS/s: under 5 s, under 1 GB (as m1 and m4).

## Gates (pre-registered)
- G1 `test_iq_measure.py` exits 0 with the new checks, which fail on the pre-fix code.
- G2 `test_iq_tools.py`, `test_control_more.py` exit 0 (the other suites that import `iqmeasure`).
- G3 the planner re-times the real DVR (not delegated): under 25 s and under 600 MB, class `carrier`,
  `power_dbm` -59.97 +/- 0.1.
- One author, two reviewers; any [high] is probe-verified before merge.
