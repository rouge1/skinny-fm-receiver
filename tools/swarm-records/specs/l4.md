# Phase l4 spec: several LTE cells on one carrier (`iqlte.cells`)

Goal: a carrier that two or more cells share (sectors of one tower, or neighbouring towers on the same frequency)
gives one entry per cell: its PCI, its frame timing and a chance estimate, instead of the strongest cell or none. On
real air (Band 14 and Band 17 carriers) the fingerprint flipped between cells and often gave no PCI. `tools/iq-lte`
then decodes each cell's broadcast (MIB, SIB1) at that cell's own timing. Numpy only in the repo. Nothing here reads
anything but the broadcast channel (MIB and the SI-RNTI SIB1), as before.

## What cells look like
- Sectors of one tower are time-synchronised: their PSS peaks sit at nearly the same timing (within a few samples)
  and have different roots (N_ID(2) = PCI mod 3 differs between neighbouring sectors). Different towers on the same
  carrier are not synchronised: their peaks sit at different timings and may share a root.
- A weaker cell must be found under a stronger one. The PSS of cells with different roots is nearly orthogonal;
  cells with the same root and different timing show as separate peaks in the folded correlation.
- A cell's SSS must be read at that cell's PSS timing, with that cell's root.

## Interfaces
- `iqlte.cells(iq, fs, max_cells=4) -> list` of dicts, strongest first, each:
  `dict(pci, nid1, nid2, frame_offset_samples, half_frame, relative_power_db (0.0 for the strongest, negative
  below), score, chance, detail)`. Only cells whose SSS chance is at most 1e-3 appear (a PCI is never guessed); a
  cell whose PSS is found but whose SSS is not appears in `iqlte.cells_weak` is NOT required: omit it. Empty list for
  noise, under 20 ms, or under 1.92 MS/s: no exception.
- `iqlte.fingerprint`: the dict gains `cells` (the list above); `pci` stays the strongest cell's (as before).
- `tools/iq-lte`: JSON gains `cells` (the same list) and, with `--decode`, each cell dict gains `decode` (the same
  shape as the top-level `decode`, or `{"ran": false, "reason": ...}`).

## Acceptance targets (measured by the tests, ok/FAIL lines)
- A: two cells, 10 MHz, native rate, SNR 20 dB, from these PCI pairs: (61, 288), (0, 1), (12, 300), (3, 4), (410, 61),
  (500, 7); relative power 0 dB and -6 dB; delay `sync` (0 samples) and `async` (a seeded random 40000-150000
  samples); seeds 0-1: 6 x 2 x 2 x 2 = 48 cases. Both PCIs reported (`cells` contains both).
- B: the same pairs at -10 dB (weak cell 10 dB under), `async` and `sync`, seed 0 (12 cases): the strong PCI reported;
  no wrong PCI reported (any reported PCI must be one of the two).
- C: three cells (PCIs 90, 91, 92: three sectors, `sync`, powers 0, -3, -6 dB), seeds 0-2 (3 cases): all three
  reported in at least 2 of 3 cases.
- D: single cell regression: the l2 cases (10 MHz, PCI in {0, 1, 2, 3, 123, 250, 251, 500, 503}, SNR 20 and 0 dB,
  seeds 0-2): `cells` has exactly one entry with that PCI in at least 52 of 54 cases; and `fingerprint`'s `pci` is
  unchanged from before in all 54.
- E: negatives (noise, tone, wifi_like, atsc_like, uplink_like, seeds 0-4): `cells == []` in 25 of 25. A 5 ms capture
  and a 1 MS/s capture: `cells == []`, no exception.
- F: time of `cells` on 0.3 s of two cells at 15.36 MS/s.

## Gates (pre-registered)
- G1 A: both PCIs reported in at least 44 of 48 cases.
- G2 B: the strong PCI reported in 12 of 12; no reported PCI outside the pair in 12 of 12.
- G3 C: all three PCIs reported in at least 2 of 3.
- G4 D: exactly one entry, the right PCI, in at least 52 of 54; `fingerprint`'s `pci` unchanged in 54 of 54.
- G5 E: `cells == []` in 25 of 25 negatives; both short captures 2 of 2, no exception.
- G6 F: under 10 s on this machine (load below 0.75 x cores; the manager measures with `time`).
- G7 `iqlte.py`, `iqlte_decode.py` and `iq-lte` import only numpy, the standard library and `fm_receiver`; no file
  outside the ownership table changes; every existing suite and `run_all.py` pass; `test_iq_lte.py` keeps its 50
  checks and passes (the stub decode of a two-cell file: each cell decoded at its own offset, `decode` per cell).
- G8 (the planner, not delegated, real air; real values are kept outside the repo): on the recorded Band 14 capture,
  `cells` reports the PCI already found for it and a second PCI (with SSS chance at most 1e-3) in at least 3 of 6
  segments of 0.3 s; on the recorded Band 17 capture, the PCI already found; on the recorded Band 13 capture (one
  cell): exactly one cell with the PCI already found in 5 of 5 segments; on the recorded ATSC capture: no cell.
- Bake-off rubric (0-100): gates G1-G6 passed weighted by case count 50; robustness (three cells at -10 dB; a 1 s
  capture; a 10 kHz CFO; scored by the consensus run from the entry's own output) 20; spec compliance (interface,
  numpy only, no change to the single-cell results) 15; clarity with a docstring stating the method and the wrong
  turns 15.
- Winner rule: only an entry passing G1, G3, G4 and G5 is eligible; highest score wins; ties go to fewer confirmed
  [high] review items. If none is eligible, the best gets one fix round; if it still fails, report.
- Controls: G5 and the `cells == []` for noise are the negative controls; G4's one-entry requirement guards against
  a detector that invents cells.
