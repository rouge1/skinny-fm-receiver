# Phase l1 spec: LTE verification, the structural fingerprint (`iqlte.py`)

Goal: given IQ of a block that looks like LTE, say whether it is an LTE FDD downlink, from the signal alone and
with a chance estimate per check: cyclic-prefix autocorrelation (check 2) and the PSS search (check 3) of the plan
in `knowledge/roadmap.md`, Round 25. It gives the PSS root (N_ID(2) = PCI mod 3) and the channel's FFT size. SSS,
CRS, TDD detection, the card, `fmctl` and any decoder are later stages. Numpy only in `iqlte.py`; scipy is allowed in
tests and the generator. Nothing here decodes anything (the user's rule: classify, don't decode).

Read `CLAUDE.md`, `AGENTS.md`, `tools/fm_receiver/iqmeasure.py` (style of a numpy-only measurement module) and
`tools/tests/test_iq_measure.py` (style of a synthetic test that prints ok/FAIL lines and exits 0 or 1).

Branching: the manager commits the Gates on `main` (the tool reads `main:`), and nothing else goes to `main`.
Every worktree is made from `main`; the branches are handed to the planner to merge ("Merge: do not merge").

First command of the manager: `swarm.py phase l1 active`. Register every task WITH its model
(`swarm.py task l1 <task> queued --model <m>`). Haiku is a Claude-driver subagent (`claude-haiku-5-5`, never the
alias `haiku`): description starts `[l1:<task>:haiku]`, then `swarm.py log-run`.

## Behaviour (exact; this is what the tests assert)
Bandwidth classes (FFT size N, native sample rate, used subcarriers excluding the empty DC one):
1.4 MHz: 128, 1.92 MS/s, 72; 3: 256, 3.84, 180; 5: 512, 7.68, 300; 10: 1024, 15.36, 600; 15: 1536, 23.04, 900;
20: 2048, 30.72, 1200. Subcarrier spacing 15 kHz. Normal CP: the first symbol of each 7-symbol slot has 160*N/2048
samples of prefix, the others 144*N/2048. 20 slots per 10 ms frame.

`lte_synth.synth_lte(bw_mhz=10, n_frames=10, pci=123, snr_db=20.0, seed=0, cfo_hz=0.0, fs=None, pss=True)
-> (iq complex64, fs)`: FDD downlink, one antenna port, from the class table. PSS (Zadoff-Chu roots 25, 29, 34 for
N_ID(2) = 0, 1, 2; 36.211 6.11.1) in the last symbol of slots 0 and 10, the central 62 of the used subcarriers; SSS
(36.211 6.11.2) in the symbol before it, subframes 0 and 5; CRS (36.211 6.10.1, port 0, v_shift = PCI mod 6, the
Gold-sequence QPSK) in the usual symbols and spacing; every other used resource element random QPSK; the PBCH may be
random QPSK. Power normalised to 1; white complex noise added so that signal power over noise power is `snr_db` at the
output sample rate; `cfo_hz` multiplies by a complex exponential. `fs` other than native resamples by a rational
factor with scipy (the output rate is exactly `fs`). `pss=False` zeroes the PSS (a control). The frame starts at
sample 0 plus a seeded random offset, returned as no value (the detector must find it).

`lte_negatives`: `synth_noise`, `synth_tone`, `synth_wifi_like`, `synth_atsc_like`, `synth_uplink_like`
(`seed=0, fs=20e6, dur=0.1` -> `(complex64, fs)`), as in the draft.

`iqlte.cp_autocorr(iq, fs)`, `iqlte.pss_search(iq, fs)`, `iqlte.fingerprint(iq, fs)`:
- each check returns a dict with at least `score` and `chance` (the probability that noise, or a signal without the
  structure, gives a score this high, honestly computed from the capture's own statistics; floored at 1e-300);
- `fingerprint` returns `dict(checks=[{name, ran, passed, score, chance, detail}, ...], verdict, pci, nid2, nfft,
  chance)`; `verdict` is one of `lte` (both checks pass at 1e-3), `possible` (one does), `not-lte`, `unknown`
  (capture shorter than 10 ms, or sample rate under 1.92 MS/s: no exception); `pci` is None this phase; `nid2` is the
  PSS root (0-2) or None; `nfft` is the FFT size of the channel's bandwidth class (1024 for a 10 MHz carrier however
  it was sampled), or None; SSS and CRS appear as checks with `ran: False`;
- the carrier is assumed centred in the capture (documented in the docstring); normal CP only; a sample clock error
  of a few ppm and a CFO up to 5 kHz are tolerated;
- `iqlte.py` imports only numpy and the standard library.

## Acceptance targets (measured by `test_iqlte.py`, printed as ok/FAIL lines)
- A: 10 MHz LTE, native rate, 10 frames; PCI in {0, 123, 503}; SNR in {20, 0} dB; seeds 0-4 (30 cases).
- B: bandwidths 1.4, 3, 5, 10, 20 MHz at 20 dB, PCI 123, 3 seeds (15 cases).
- C: 10 MHz LTE, resampled to 20 MS/s, CFO +2 kHz, SNR 10 dB, PCI 123, seeds 0-4 (5 cases).
- D: negatives noise, tone, wifi_like, atsc_like, uplink_like, seeds 0-4 (25 cases).
- E: the control `pss=False` at 20 dB, seeds 0-4 (5 cases); a 5 ms capture (3 cases).
- F: time for `fingerprint` on 0.1 s at 15.36 MS/s.

## Gates (pre-registered: fixed before any worker runs)
- G1 A: `verdict == 'lte'` and `nid2 == pci % 3` and `nfft == 1024` in 30 of 30 cases.
- G2 B: `verdict == 'lte'` and `nfft` equals the class table's N in 15 of 15 cases.
- G3 C: `verdict == 'lte'` and `nid2 == 0` (123 % 3) in 5 of 5 cases.
- G4 D: `verdict != 'lte'` in 25 of 25 cases; and for `synth_noise` both checks' `chance` >= 0.01 in 5 of 5 cases.
- G5 E: the control `pss=False` gives `verdict != 'lte'` in 5 of 5; the 5 ms capture gives `unknown` with no exception
  in 3 of 3.
- G6 F: under 5 s on this machine, measured by the manager with `time`, load below 0.75 x cores.
- G7 in the cases of G1 at 20 dB, both checks' `chance` <= 1e-6.
- G8 `iqlte.py` imports only numpy and the standard library (the test reads the file's imports); no file outside the
  ownership table is changed; `run_all.py` passes in full with the new suite in it (the manager shows the output).
- G9 the planner (not delegated) runs the winning `iqlte.py` on `recordings/fm-581.00MHz-20261016-080127-iq-band.cfile`
  (the ATSC capture, resampled by the planner): `verdict != 'lte'`.
- Bake-off rubric (0-100): gates passed, weighted by case count 50; robustness (the same cases at -5 dB SNR, and a
  1 s capture, scored by the consensus run from the entry's own output) 20; spec compliance (interface, numpy only,
  `unknown` handling) 15; clarity, with a docstring stating the method and the wrong turns, 15.
- Winner rule: only an entry that passes G1-G5 is eligible; highest score wins; a tie goes to the entry with fewer
  confirmed [high] review items. If none is eligible, the best entry gets one fix round; if it still fails, report.
- Controls: G4 and G5 are the negative controls; a detector that says `lte` for everything fails them.
